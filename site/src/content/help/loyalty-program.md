## Loyalty program

Shoppers earn points for paid orders, creating an account and more, and spend them on rewards: an amount or percent off,
free shipping, a free product or store credit. Members see their points in the rewards button on every page, on product
pages ("Earn 34 points with this"), in the cart, on the Rewards page, in their customer account and on the thank you page.

The program is on the **Growth** and **Pro** plans. Pro adds VIP tiers, bonus campaigns, product and collection rates,
cashback, affiliate codes, Klaviyo, Omnisend, Judge.me and Shopify Flow, and sending emails from your own address.

## Set up (Loyalty in the app)

1. **Choose what earns points** on **Earn points**: points per 1 of your currency on paid orders (with or without
   shipping and taxes), extra points on the first order, points for creating an account, birthdays, social follows and
   product reviews. Customers with a tag you list (for example wholesale) can't join.
2. **Add rewards** on **Rewards**: what members spend their points on, how many points each costs and how many days a
   reward code stays valid.
3. **Turn the program on** on **Loyalty home** and choose which orders count when it starts (from now on, also the last
   30 days, or also the last 90 days). Turning it off stops earning; members keep their points.
4. **Show the widget**: turn on the **Conversion boosters** embed in your theme (App embeds). The rewards button shows on
   every page. For the Rewards page, add the **Order & wishlist pages** block to a page template.
5. **Make it yours** on **Look**: colours, where the button sits, and every word the widget and the Rewards page show.

Points from an order count once it is paid. A refund or cancellation takes them back. New points can stay pending for some days
first (14 by default), so an order that is returned never gives points to spend.

## Referrals

On **Referrals**, members share their own link: a friend gets a discount on their first order, and the member gets a
gift once that order is paid. Fraud rules limit how many referrals a member is rewarded for in 30 days. On Pro,
**affiliate codes** count the sales of creators and partners (you pay their commission yourself).

## Emails

On **Emails** the app tells members what they earned and when a reward is ready: welcome, points earned, reward ready,
reward code, store credit added, birthday and points about to expire, among others. Reminders (birthday, points about to
expire, the monthly summary) only go to members who agreed to email marketing, and every email has an unsubscribe link
and your store's address. On Pro you can send from your own address.

## Members and analytics

**Members** lists everyone in the program with their points and history; you can add or take away points, and export or
import members as a CSV file. **Analytics** shows members over time, points earned and spent, the rewards redeemed most and referrals.

## Plan limits

Growth includes 500 orders that earn points and 1,000 loyalty emails a month; Pro includes 2,000 and 5,000. At a limit,
orders stop earning and emails wait until next month, unless you turn on **Allow extra** under the usage bar
(Loyalty home, Emails or Settings): then they go on, and the extra is added to your Shopify bill. The bars show how much
of each limit is used.
