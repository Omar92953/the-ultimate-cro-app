## Announcement bar and countdown timers

Messages rotate at the top of the store, each with an icon, a link and optional start and end dates, plus free shipping progress if you want. Countdown timers can sit in the header, footer, home page, product pages and cart, each with its own design, counting to a fixed date, a daily cut off or a time per visitor. Use real deadlines only.

## Conversion boosters

Sales pop ups, trust badges, stock urgency and sticky add to cart run from the **Conversion boosters** app embed; set them on the Boosters page.

- **Sales pop ups:** recent real purchases (product and time; the city only if you turn it on; never names).
- **Trust badges:** badges and payment icons under Add to cart, as a row or a grid.
- **Stock urgency:** "Only 3 left" when stock is at or below your threshold, from your real stock.
- **Sticky add to cart:** a bar that stays on screen while shoppers scroll.

## Quick add, delivery estimate, back in stock and wishlist

- **Quick add:** a button on every product card; sizes open a small picker. Design the button and the "Added to your cart" popup from its card in Storefront; in the theme editor you only switch the embed on.
- **Delivery estimate:** "Order within 2h 15m and get it Thu to Sat" from your cut off time, days off, holidays and delivery times per country.
- **Back in stock and pre order:** "Notify me" on sold out products (requests are listed in the app) and "Pre order" on products you still sell when out of stock.
- **Wishlist:** hearts on product pages and cards, a wishlist page with add to cart, price dropped badges and a share link, saved in the shopper's browser.
