## Offers in your theme's cart

In **Cart & Checkout**, the **Cart** group has everything for the cart. Switch on **Offers in your theme's cart** to show cross sell offers inside your theme's own cart drawer (Dawn and Dawn based themes).

## Gift note and delivery date

"This is a gift", a gift message and a delivery date (with time slots if you want) picked in the cart, all saved on the order.

## Free shipping bar

Set the goal and messages in **Cart & Checkout → Free shipping bar**. With **Give free shipping automatically** on (the default), saving creates or updates the automatic discount "CRO Toolbox free shipping": free shipping on orders from the goal amount. Turning the bar or the option off switches the discount off.

- **Inside the cart:** in the cart drawer and on the cart page, above the products or just above the checkout button. Needs the Conversion boosters app embed on (or the bar block in the header).
- **Bar at the top of the store:** place the block in the Header area.
