## Choose your cart

In **Cart**, first choose: keep your theme's cart and add offers to it, or use the app's cart drawer. Then switch on its modules.

## The app's cart drawer

A slide out cart that opens on add to cart and from the cart icon, with quantities, the subtotal, express checkout buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay), the best discount from codes you list, a shareable cart link and a remembered cart.

## Gift note and delivery date

"This is a gift", a gift message and a delivery date (with time slots if you want) picked in the cart, all saved on the order.

## Free shipping bar

Set the goal and messages in **Storefront → Free shipping bar**. With **Give free shipping automatically** on (the default), saving creates or updates the automatic discount "CRO Toolbox free shipping": free shipping on orders from the goal amount. Turning the bar or the option off switches the discount off.

- **Inside the cart:** in the cart drawer and on the cart page, above the products or just above the checkout button. Needs the Conversion boosters app embed on (or the bar block in the header).
- **Bar at the top of the store:** place the block in the Header area.
