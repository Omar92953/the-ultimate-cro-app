## How sections work

Make the content in the app (reviews, questions, logos, banners, slides, videos), design the look in each section's **Design** page, then click **Add to theme** and place the block in the theme editor. Every copy of a section uses its design; a copy can pick another of your styles.

## Reviews

Add text, photo and video reviews with their source (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Google). Shoppers can write reviews that you approve. Show them as a carousel, a grid, masonry or a full list, and on product pages as a star rating, a snippet card or a full list.

## Logos and FAQ

Logos or short texts scroll in a strip or sit in a grid. FAQ questions are grouped with buttons and a search box; a FAQ copy can show only one group (for example Shipping), have its own heading, and switch Google's FAQ data on or off.

## Hero banners

Make banners in **Storefront → Hero banners**: a wide desktop image and a tall phone image, heading, text, up to two buttons, where the text sits (separately for phones), colours and height. In the theme editor add the **Hero image** section and pick a banner. Set **Where on the page** to "Lower down" for banners that aren't at the top, so they load only when scrolled to.

## Image and video carousels

Image carousel slides each have a picture, title, text, button and link. For the video carousel, upload videos in Content → Files (or to a product's media), add them in the app, set the order, add captions and link each to a product; linked videos show the product name, price and an Add to cart button. **Video carousel → Style** also sets the pages it shows on.

## Quiz and collection pills

The quiz asks a few questions whose answers point to product tags, then shows the best matching products with add to cart. Collection pills are a row of buttons to your collections, with the current one highlighted.
