## Discounts

**Offers → Discounts** has three kinds:

- **Buy X get Y:** buy some products, get others (or the same) free or at a discount.
- **Volume discounts:** tiers by quantity, shown on the product page.
- **Gift with purchase:** a free gift once the cart reaches an amount.

Shopify applies them at checkout through the app's automatic discount.

## Add ons

Extras ticked under Add to cart, such as gift wrapping, added together with the product, plus an optional gift message saved on the order. Choose a list or cards, your heading and colours, and where it shows (all products, specific products or collections). Add ons are never ticked in advance.

## Personalization

Text fields (for example a name to engrave) with a live preview on the product picture, colour choices, required fields, a character limit and an optional fee. The choices are saved on the order with the item.
