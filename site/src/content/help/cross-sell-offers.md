## Create a cross sell offer

- **Trigger:** all products, specific products, or products in collections. On the cart page and in the drawer, the trigger is "this product is in the cart".
- **Offered products:** sold out products, the product being viewed and products already in the cart are skipped automatically.
- **Where it appears:** product page, cart page and/or cart drawer.
- **Discount (optional):** a percentage off the offered products. It applies at checkout while a trigger product is also in the cart, however the products were added.

In the theme editor you can also choose **Shopify recommendations** for products that no rule covers.

## Offers in your theme's cart

Heading, how many products and the button text are on **Cross sell offers → Style** (Content tab). Offers in the theme's own cart drawer work with Dawn and Dawn based themes. If yours is different, use the app's cart drawer (Cart).
