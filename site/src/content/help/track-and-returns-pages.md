## Track order and returns pages

- **Track order:** shoppers look up their order with the order number and email and see its delivery status.
- **Returns:** shoppers ask for a return with the order number and email, choose items and a reason; requests are listed in the app (Returns).

## Set up (Tracking & Returns in the app)

1. **Approve the returns permission** when the app asks (open the app from Shopify's Apps list).
2. **Make the two pages.** Click **Make the pages**: the app creates "Track your order" and "Returns" in your store.
3. **Add the block once.** Click **Open the theme editor**, choose Add block, CRO Toolbox, Order & wishlist pages, and save. With Page set to Automatic it shows Track order on the first page, Returns on the second, and nothing on any other page.
4. **Set your return rules and address**, then add both pages to your menu or footer.

Each step turns green when it is done. Each page has ready made looks (Style).
