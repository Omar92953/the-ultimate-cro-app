## What Analytics shows

Sales from the app's offers and widgets over the last 30 days compared with the 30 before, results for each offer and each bundle, and the holdout test.

## The holdout test

A share of shoppers sees no app widgets, so you can compare what shoppers with and without them spend. It is on the Growth plan and above.
