## Payment and delivery rules

Hide, rename or move payment methods and delivery options at checkout, with conditions such as the order amount, countries, products or customer type. For example: hide Cash on Delivery for orders over $100.

## Order limits

Minimum or maximum order total, items in the order or quantity per product, each with your own message at checkout.

## Thank you page

Modules after checkout: a discount code for the next order with a copy button, a message, follow us buttons and product suggestions. Works on every Shopify plan.

## After purchase offer

One product at a discount right after payment, added to the order with one click and no new payment details. Choose the product, the discount and a minimum order amount. The accept and decline buttons use Shopify's fixed wording.
