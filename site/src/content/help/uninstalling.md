You can uninstall CRO Toolbox at any time. Nothing locks you in.

## How to uninstall

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings → Apps** (called **Apps and sales channels** in some admins).
2. Find **CRO Toolbox** and open it, or use the three dots next to it.
3. Choose **Uninstall** and confirm.

## What happens to your store

- The offers, bundles, bars, reviews, cart drawer and other app features stop showing, because the app is no longer there to run them.
- The checkout discounts, bundle splitting and checkout rules (payment, delivery and order limits) from the app stop working.
- Your theme keeps empty leftovers: app blocks and embeds you added. They show nothing. To tidy up, open **Online Store → Themes → Customize** and remove the CRO Toolbox blocks and embeds.
- Products and pages the app created for you, such as a mix and match bundle product or a bundle page, stay in your store as normal products and pages. Archive or delete them if you no longer want them.
- Orders already placed are not changed.

## Your plan and billing

Uninstalling stops the app's billing. Shopify ends the subscription when the app is removed, so you are not charged for the next period. Your Shopify invoice shows any charges up to the day you uninstalled. See [plans and billing](/help/plans-and-billing/).

## Your data

48 hours after you uninstall, Shopify asks us to delete your store's data and we do: its sessions, back in stock requests, return requests and feature requests. Data the app saved inside your store is removed by Shopify together with the app. Details are in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Coming back

Install the app again from the Shopify App Store. Because the store's data is deleted after uninstalling, you start from a clean setup: follow [getting started](/help/getting-started/) and [turn on the app embeds](/help/turn-on-app-embeds/) again.

## Before you go

If something was not working, tell us at [support](/support/).
