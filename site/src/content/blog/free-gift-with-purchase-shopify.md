A free gift with purchase gives shoppers a free product once their order reaches a threshold: a cart total, such as a free tote on orders over $50, or a number of items, such as buy 2, get 1 free. It works best when the gift is useful, the threshold is a little above your typical order and shoppers can see how close they are. In CRO Toolbox both kinds live in **Offers → Discounts**, and Shopify applies them at checkout.

## Spend threshold or buy threshold?

There are two ways to give something free, and they suit different goals.

- **A spend threshold** (**Gift with purchase** in the app): a free gift once the cart total reaches an amount. It raises order value across the whole store, because any product counts towards it. You can also limit it to carts that have certain products.
- **A buy threshold** (**Buy X get Y** in the app): buy a number of items, get others free or at a percentage off. It suits a single product or a range: "Buy 2 shirts, get 1 free", or "Buy a camera, get the case 50% off".

They also behave differently in the cart. With **Gift with purchase**, the app adds the gift to the cart itself once the shopper qualifies, and removes it if the cart drops below the amount. With **Buy X get Y**, the shopper adds the items, and the cheapest "get" items are discounted automatically at checkout.

## How do I pick the threshold?

Start from your typical order: the order total most of your orders sit around. Set the threshold a little above it, so most shoppers are one small item away. For example, if most orders are around $40, a gift from $50 asks for one more product, while a gift from $120 asks for too much and few shoppers will try.

Two things to check before you choose:

- **The cost.** The gift costs you the product and, often, a little more shipping weight. Work out what is left on an order right at the threshold.
- **Other goals.** If you already have a free shipping goal, put the gift above it, so each goal is a separate next step. The free shipping bar can show both (see below).

In the app the amount does not count the gift itself, and shoppers paying in other currencies get the converted amount.

## How do I choose the gift?

- **Useful with what you sell.** A travel size, a refill, a cleaning cloth, a tote. Something that makes the main purchase better.
- **Low cost to you, clear value to the shopper.** A sample of a product they haven't tried can also lead to a second order.
- **In stock.** Pick a gift you have enough of for the whole promotion.
- **One product option.** In the app the gift is one specific variant, for example the 30 ml size in one scent, so there is nothing for the shopper to choose.
- **Not your best seller at full size.** Giving that away teaches shoppers to wait for the gift.

## How do shoppers see their progress?

A threshold only works if shoppers know about it. With a **Gift with purchase** offer, product pages and the cart page show a card with the gift's picture, a progress bar and a message such as "Spend $12 more to get Tote bag free". Once the cart reaches the amount, it changes to "You've unlocked a free Tote bag!" and the gift is added to the cart. If you limit the gift to certain products, product pages show the card only on those products.

You can also show the gift as a step on the free shipping bar:

1. Open **Cart & Checkout → Free shipping bar** and go to **Reward steps**.
2. Click **Add a reward step** and set **Reward** to **Free gift**.
3. Set the amount in **From**, the **Label on the bar**, and the messages for **On the way** and **Reached**.
4. Set up the gift itself as a **Gift with purchase** offer with the same amount.

The bar then shows each step, and its message always points to the next one. The [cart help guide](/help/cart/) explains where the bar shows.

## How do I set up a free gift in CRO Toolbox?

1. Open **Offers → Discounts**, choose the **Gift with purchase** tab and click **Create gift offer**.
2. Under **Summary**, write the **Name**. Shoppers see it on the product page and next to the discount at checkout.
3. Under **1. The gift**, click **Choose gift** and pick the product option.
4. Under **2. When shoppers get it**, set **Cart total at least**. If the gift should only come with certain products, tick **Only when the cart has certain products** and choose them. This takes specific products, not collections.
5. Switch on **Active** and save.
6. Check that the **Conversion boosters** app embed is on in your theme; the progress card needs it. See [turn on the app embeds](/help/turn-on-app-embeds/).
7. Test it: add products below the amount and check the progress, go over it and check that the gift appears and is free at checkout, then remove a product and check that the gift goes.

## How do I set up buy X get Y?

1. Open **Offers → Discounts**, choose the **Buy X get Y** tab and click **Create buy X get Y offer**.
2. Under **1. Customer buys**, set the **Quantity** and the products in **Of**.
3. Under **2. Customer gets**, set the **Quantity**. Keep **From the same products** ticked for deals like "Buy 2, get 1 free", where of every 3 items the cheapest is discounted. Untick it to choose other products in **From**.
4. In **At**, choose **Free** or **A percentage off** and set the discount.
5. Write the **Name**, switch on **Active** and save.
6. Place a test order with enough items and check the price at checkout.

Qualifying product pages show the deal, for example "Add 3 to your cart: 1 of them free". The [discounts help guide](/help/discounts-addons-personalization/) covers all three discount types, including volume discounts.

## Can I combine a gift with other offers?

Yes, with care. All of the app's discounts run through one automatic discount called "CRO Toolbox offers", and you can change which other discounts it combines with in Shopify's Discounts. A gift with purchase sits well next to a cross sell or [quantity breaks](/blog/quantity-breaks-that-raise-aov/) on another product. Avoid a gift, a quantity break and a discount code all on the same product: shoppers can't tell what they will pay.

## Which plan do I need?

Buy X get Y, volume discounts and gift with purchase are on the Free plan, within its 3 offers on at once. Starter, at $5.99 a month, has unlimited offers. ## Quick checklist

- A spend threshold for store wide order value, a buy threshold for one product or range
- The threshold a little above your typical order, and above your free shipping goal
- A useful, low cost gift with enough stock, as one product option
- Progress shown on product pages and in the cart, or as a step on the free shipping bar
- A test order below and above the amount, and with the gift removed
