An upsell offers the shopper more of the product they chose or a better version of it: two instead of one, a bigger size, a premium option. A cross sell offers a different product that goes with it, like a case with a phone or a cream with a serum. Both raise what one order is worth, and most stores use both, in different places.

## What is an upsell?

An upsell keeps the shopper on the product they already want and gives them a reason to take more of it, or a better version. The decision is small: "do I want two?" or "is the bigger size worth it?" rather than "do I want something else?".

Common upsells on Shopify:

- **Quantity breaks:** Buy 2, save 10%; Buy 3, save 15%.
- **Size upgrades:** a saving on a bigger size, for example L saves 10%.
- **Upgrade offers:** swap the chosen option for a better one, such as the 3 pack instead of the single, for the price difference.
- **An offer after payment:** one more product at a discount, added to the paid order in one click.

## What is a cross sell?

A cross sell answers "what else do I need?". It suggests a product that completes the one in front of the shopper: matches with a candle, a filter with a coffee maker, socks with shoes. The shopper adds a second product rather than more of the first.

Common cross sells on Shopify:

- **Pairs well with offers** under the buy button, where the shopper ticks the items they want.
- **Frequently bought together boxes**, which show a small set with one button to add all of it.
- **Cart offers**, which suggest one or two items that go with what is already in the cart.

## Upsell vs cross sell: examples by product

The same store can usually do both. A few examples:

- **Skincare serum.** Upsell: the 50 ml size instead of 30 ml for the difference. Cross sell: the matching moisturiser.
- **Basic tees.** Upsell: Buy 3, save 15%, with a different size for each tee. Cross sell: socks or a belt.
- **Coffee beans.** Upsell: the 1 kg bag instead of 250 g. Cross sell: paper filters or a mug.
- **Candles.** Upsell: Buy 2, save 10%. Cross sell: a wick trimmer or matches.
- **Phone.** Upsell: more storage. Cross sell: a case and a screen protector.

The test is simple: if the offer is the same product (more of it, or a better version), it's an upsell. If it's a different product that goes with it, it's a cross sell.

## Which one should I use first?

It depends on what you sell.

- **Products people use up or buy in multiples** (consumables, basics, gifts): start with an upsell. Quantity breaks are easy to understand and need no extra product.
- **Products that need something else to work well** (devices, tools, anything with refills or accessories): start with a cross sell.
- **A catalogue of small, related items:** a frequently bought together box or another bundle may fit better than either. See [Shopify bundles](/shopify-bundles/).

Neither is a good fit when the offer doesn't make sense for the product. A quantity break on something people buy once, like a mattress, or a cross sell of an unrelated best seller, adds clutter without helping the shopper.

## Where should each one show?

- **Upsells** belong on the product page, next to Add to cart, because they change what the shopper is about to add. An upgrade offer can also show in the cart.
- **Cross sells** work on the product page, on the cart page and in the cart drawer, where the shopper is looking at their order.
- **The after purchase offer** shows right after payment, when the order is already safe.

One clear offer per spot is easier to read than several. On a product page, that often means one upsell above the buy button and one cross sell below it, not three boxes stacked together.

## How do I set up an upsell with CRO Toolbox?

CRO Toolbox is a [Shopify upsell app](/shopify-upsell-app/) as well as a cross sell app. For quantity breaks:

1. Open **Offers → Upsell offers** and create an offer.
2. Choose which products get it: all products, specific products or products in collections.
3. Add tiers: quantity, discount and an optional label and badge, for example "Most popular".
4. Open **Style** to pick a ready made look and adjust wording, colours, corners and spacing with the live preview.
5. Click **Add to theme** and place the block above your Add to cart button.

The tier the shopper picks sets the quantity on your normal Add to cart button. At checkout the discount applies to that product once the cart reaches the tier's quantity, and different variants of the same product count together. For a bigger size instead, set **Upsell type** to **Size upgrade**. For apparel, keep **Let shoppers pick a size for each item** on, so Buy 2 can be one M and one L. Details: [upsell offers and quantity breaks](/help/upsell-offers/) and [quantity breaks that raise average order value](/blog/quantity-breaks-that-raise-aov/).

## How do I set up a cross sell with CRO Toolbox?

1. Open **Offers → Cross sell offers** and create an offer.
2. Choose the trigger: all products, specific products, or products in collections. On the cart page and in the drawer, the trigger is "this product is in the cart".
3. Pick the products to offer. Sold out products, the product being viewed and products already in the cart are skipped automatically.
4. Choose where it appears: product page, cart page and/or cart drawer.
5. Add an optional discount: a percentage off the offered products.
6. Set the look in **Style**, then place the block in the theme editor and save.

The discount applies at checkout while a trigger product is also in the cart, however the products were added. Nothing is ticked in advance: the shopper picks what they want. Offers inside your theme's own cart drawer work with Dawn and Dawn based themes; for other themes, use the app's own cart drawer (**Cart**) or the cart page. Details: [cross sell offers](/help/cross-sell-offers/) and the [cross sell feature page](/features/cross-sell/).

For a set shown as one box with a single button, use **Bundles → Create bundle offer** and choose **Frequently bought together**. The box shows on the product page of every item in the set. More on that in [Shopify cross sell and frequently bought together](/shopify-cross-sell/).

## Should the offer have a discount?

Not always. An upsell usually needs one, because "Buy 2" without a saving gives no reason to take two. A cross sell can work without one when the item is genuinely needed, like filters with a coffee maker. Start with small discounts and only add more if shoppers don't take the offer.

## Which plan do I need?

Upsell offers and cross sell offers are both on the Free plan, which allows 3 offers on at once and 50 orders a month. Paid plans start with Starter at $5.99 a month, with unlimited offers on at once. A/B tests for upsells and cross sells are on Pro at $39.99 a month. See [pricing](/pricing/).

## How do I know which one works?

Change one thing at a time and compare average order value over the same number of days before and after. CRO Toolbox's **Analytics** shows sales from each offer. On Growth ($19.99 a month) and above, the **holdout test** keeps a share of shoppers without app widgets, so you can compare what shoppers with and without them spend.

## Quick checklist

- Upsell: same product, more of it or a better version
- Cross sell: a different product that goes with it
- Upsells next to Add to cart, cross sells under it and in the cart
- One clear offer per spot
- Small discounts first, nothing ticked in advance
- A test order to check the price at checkout
