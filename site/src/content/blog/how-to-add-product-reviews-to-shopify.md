To add product reviews to Shopify, install a reviews app, then place three things on the product page in the theme editor: a star rating line next to the price, one short review near the Add to cart button, and the full list of reviews lower down with a Write a review button. Shopify retired its own free Product Reviews app in 2024 and most themes have no built in review form, so an app does the collecting and the showing. Here is where each piece goes, how to get your first reviews, and what to check before you choose an app.

## Where should reviews go on a product page?

Three places, each doing a different job:

- **A star rating next to the price.** The shopper sees it in the first second, before scrolling. It answers "do other people like this?" at a glance.
- **One review near the Add to cart button.** A short, specific review where the shopper decides. Pick one that answers a common doubt, such as fit, taste or how long delivery took.
- **The full list further down.** For shoppers who want to read more, with photos first and a Write a review button.

Make the star rating a link that scrolls to the full list, so curious shoppers can jump straight to it.

## How do I add reviews to my Shopify theme?

The steps are the same with most reviews apps:

1. Install the app and import or add your first reviews.
2. Open **Online Store → Themes → Customize** and go to the product page template.
3. Add the app's star rating block under the product title or price.
4. Add the review card block near the buy buttons, and the full list block lower on the page.
5. Save, then open a product with reviews and one without, to check both look right.

Many apps also have a section for the home page: a carousel or grid of your best reviews from across the store.

## How do I get my first reviews?

- **Ask customers after delivery.** A short message a few days after the order arrives, with a link to the product, gets more replies than one sent at checkout.
- **Use the reviews you already have.** Kind messages on WhatsApp or Instagram, comments on TikTok and Google reviews are real reviews. Ask the customer if you can show them, and show where each one came from.
- **Let shoppers write one on the product page,** and approve each review before it shows, so spam never reaches your store.

Never write reviews yourself or pay for fake ones. It breaks consumer protection rules in many countries and shoppers spot it quickly.

## Do photo and video reviews matter?

Yes, for products where looks, size or texture matter: clothing, beauty, home and food. A photo from a real customer shows the product in normal light, on a normal person, and answers questions your own pictures can't. Show reviews with photos first in the list, and let shoppers open a photo large.

## Can reviews show stars in Google?

A reviews app can add review data to the product page that Google can read. Google decides whether to show stars in its results, so treat stars in search as a bonus, not a promise.

## What should I check before choosing a reviews app?

- **Where the reviews show:** a star rating, a single review card and a full list on the product page, plus a section for the home page.
- **Photo and video reviews,** and a way to add reviews from WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok or Google with their source.
- **Approval before reviews go live.**
- **The look:** the reviews should match your store's fonts and colours.
- **What it costs as you grow:** some apps charge by orders or by review requests each month.
- **Page speed:** add the blocks on a test copy of your theme and check the product page still loads quickly.

## Quick checklist

- A star rating next to the price, linked to the full list
- One short, specific review near Add to cart
- The full list lower on the page, photos first
- A Write a review button, with approval before reviews show
- Real reviews from messages and social media, with their source
- A message to customers a few days after delivery asking for a review
- The product page checked on a phone after adding the blocks
