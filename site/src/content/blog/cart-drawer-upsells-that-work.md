The cart drawer is a good place for an upsell because the shopper has already decided to buy. What works there is small: one or two inexpensive items that complete what's in the cart, shown with a clear add button and never added without asking. Here is how to choose those items and set them up on Shopify.

## Why upsell in the cart drawer?

The drawer opens right after add to cart, when the shopper is looking at their order. A short, relevant suggestion at that moment feels helpful rather than pushy. It also sits next to the subtotal, which makes a free shipping goal easy to see.

## What should I offer?

- **Complements, not alternatives.** Batteries with the toy, a refill with the device, socks with the shoes.
- **Lower prices than the main item.** An add on under a third of the cart value is an easy yes.
- **Things in stock.** CRO Toolbox skips sold out products, products already in the cart and the product being viewed.
- **One or two offers.** More than that turns the drawer into a catalogue.

## Should the offer have a discount?

It can, but it doesn't have to. A small percentage off works when the item is often bought together with what's in the cart. In CRO Toolbox the cross sell discount applies at checkout while a trigger product is in the cart.

## Which drawer does it work in?

CRO Toolbox adds offers to **your theme's own cart drawer** with **Offers in your theme's cart**. This works with Dawn and Dawn based themes. If your theme's drawer is not supported, the same offers can show on the cart page.

## How do I set it up?

1. In the app, open **Storefront** and turn on **Offers in your theme's cart** (the Cart group).
2. Create a cross sell offer (**Offers → Cross sell offers**) with the trigger products and the products to offer, and include the cart drawer in where it appears.
3. Turn on the free shipping bar and set the goal a little above your typical order.
4. Add something to the cart on your store and check the drawer.

## What makes a cart upsell feel pushy?

- Items ticked in advance. CRO Toolbox never ticks offers for the shopper.
- Pop ups on top of the drawer.
- Offers unrelated to the cart.
- Too many items.

Keep it to one row, one clear button and an honest price.

## How do I know it works?

Track how often the offered items are added from the drawer, and average order value. CRO Toolbox's **Analytics** shows sales from each offer; the **holdout test** on Growth and above compares shoppers with and without the app's widgets.
