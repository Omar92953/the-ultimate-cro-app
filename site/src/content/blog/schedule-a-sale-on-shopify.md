To schedule a sale on Shopify, give the discount a start date and an end date: Shopify's own discounts (codes and automatic discounts) have both in **Discounts**, and the sale then starts and ends by itself. Offers made in an app, such as a quantity break, a bundle or a free gift, need their own schedule, and CRO Toolbox has one on the Pro plan. Below is how to plan a scheduled sale, what to set in Shopify, and how to schedule offers with CRO Toolbox.

## Why schedule a sale instead of switching it on by hand?

- **It starts on time**, even at midnight or while you sleep.
- **It ends on time**, so a weekend price doesn't run into Tuesday.
- **Every part moves together**: the discount, the announcement bar and the offers can all switch at the same moment.
- **The end is real.** A countdown that matches a real end time is honest urgency; a timer that resets is not (see [countdown timers without fake urgency](/blog/countdown-timers-without-fake-urgency/)).

## How do I schedule a discount in Shopify?

Shopify's own discounts have dates built in:

1. In Shopify admin, open **Discounts** and create a discount code or an automatic discount.
2. Set what it gives: an amount or percent off products or the order, Buy X get Y, or free shipping.
3. Under **Active dates**, set the start date and time, tick **Set end date** and set the end.
4. Save. Shopify shows it as Scheduled until it starts.

Times use your store's time zone, set in **Settings → General**. Check it before a sale that is announced for a time of day.

## Which offers can be part of a scheduled sale?

A sale is more than a price cut. Offers that raise the order value can run for the same days:

- **Quantity breaks**, such as Buy 2, save 10%, for products people buy more than one of.
- **Bundles**, a set at one price for gift seasons.
- **A free gift** once the cart reaches an amount.
- **Buy X get Y**, for clearing stock.
- **Cross sells**, products that go with what is in the cart.

See [quantity breaks that raise AOV](/blog/quantity-breaks-that-raise-aov/) and [free gift with purchase on Shopify](/blog/free-gift-with-purchase-shopify/) for how to set each one up well.

## How do I schedule offers with CRO Toolbox?

Scheduled offers are on the Pro plan ($39.99 a month). They work for upsells, cross sells, bundle offers and discounts (Buy X get Y, volume discounts and free gifts).

1. Open the offer in the app.
2. In the **Schedule** card, switch on **Start at a set time** and pick the **Start** date and time. The offer stays off until then.
3. Switch on **End at a set time** and pick the **End**. The offer switches off then.
4. Save.

Times are in your store's time zone. The app switches the offer on at the start and off at the end, once each, so if you switch it by hand in between, your choice stays. The offers list shows when each scheduled offer starts or ends. Details: [A/B tests and scheduled offers help guide](/help/ab-tests-and-scheduled-offers/).

## How should I plan a scheduled sale?

1. **Pick the dates and the time zone** and write them down in one place.
2. **Schedule the discount** in Shopify and **the offers** in the app for the same start and end.
3. **Announce it**: an [announcement bar](/features/announcements/) with the dates, and a [countdown](/features/countdown/) to the real end.
4. **Check the day before**: open the scheduled items and confirm the times.
5. **Check just after the start**: add a product to the cart on your store and go to checkout to see the discount.
6. **After the end**, confirm everything switched off and note what sold, for next time.

## Quick checklist

- Store time zone checked
- Shopify discount with start and end dates
- App offers scheduled for the same times
- Announcement and countdown matching the real end
- A test order path checked after the start
