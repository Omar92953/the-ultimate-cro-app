The best Shopify loyalty app is the one that has the parts of a program you will really use (points, rewards, referrals, perhaps VIP tiers) on a plan that fits your monthly orders. Most loyalty apps start free and keep VIP tiers, integrations and a loyalty page for higher plans, so the plan you will end up on matters more than the starting price. We make CRO Toolbox, so read this as a guide with the facts we could check on each app's own listing, including the cases where another app is the better pick.

## What should a loyalty app do?

At minimum:

- **Points for orders and a few actions**, such as creating an account or a birthday.
- **Rewards shoppers want.** Money off, free shipping, a free product or store credit.
- **Referrals**, so members bring friends.
- **Points where shoppers look.** A rewards button, product pages, the cart, the customer account, and Shopify POS if you also sell in person.
- **A fit with your other tools**, above all your email app and your reviews app.

## Questions to ask before you install

1. **Which plan has VIP tiers?** It is often the biggest price jump in the category.
2. **How are orders counted, and what happens above the limit?** Some plans stop at a monthly number, others charge for every extra 100 orders.
3. **Does it send loyalty emails**, or pass points to the email tool you already use?
4. **Can you bring your members and their points** from the app you use today?
5. **Does any part need Shopify Plus?** Several apps list redeeming points inside checkout as a Shopify Plus feature.

## What does each app offer, and who is it best for?

### Smile

The [Smile listing](https://apps.shopify.com/smile-io) shows 4.9 stars from 4,681 reviews and the Built for Shopify badge. The free plan covers up to 200 orders a month with points, rewards, referrals and Shopify POS. Essential is $15 a month (500 orders), Standard $79 (1,000 orders, bonus events, points on product pages) and Growth $199 (2,500 orders, then $20 per 100 more). VIP tiers and points expiry are listed only on Growth, together with redeeming at checkout on Shopify Plus. The listing names Klaviyo, Judge.me, Gorgias and more than 30 other integrations.

**Best for:** established stores that want the best known name in the category and many integrations, and that can budget for Growth when they want VIP tiers.

### BON Loyalty

The [BON Loyalty listing](https://apps.shopify.com/bon-loyalty-rewards) shows 5.0 stars from 1,992 reviews and the Built for Shopify badge. Its free plan covers up to 150 orders a month and already includes automated emails for points and referrals, plus redeeming at checkout on Shopify Plus. Starter is $15 (300 orders, a loyalty page, birthday rewards, points expiration), Basic $29 (500 orders, Shopify POS, multi language display) and Growth $129 with unlimited monthly orders, VIP tiers and B2B tiers. Paid plans have a 7 day free trial.

**Best for:** stores that want loyalty emails from the first day on a free or low plan, wholesale stores that need B2B tiers, and big stores that want VIP tiers with no order cap.

### Rivo

The [Rivo listing](https://apps.shopify.com/rivo-loyalty) shows 4.7 stars from 1,441 reviews. The free plan covers up to 200 orders a month with points and automated email campaigns. Essential is $15 (500 orders, a loyalty and referral page, Shopify POS). Scale is $49 for 400 orders, then $20 per 100 more, capped at $499 a month; it adds VIP tiers, points expiry, bonus campaigns and the referral program. Plus is $499 for up to 2,500 orders. Worked out from those numbers, a store with 1,000 orders a month would pay $169 on Scale.

**Best for:** fast growing direct to consumer brands that want VIP tiers below $50 at low volume, and value the 24/7 human support its plans list.

### LoyaltyLion

The [LoyaltyLion listing](https://apps.shopify.com/loyaltylion) shows 4.7 stars from 552 reviews. It is free to install, with a free plan for 400 orders a month (points, discount rewards, analytics and segments, 7 languages). Classic is $199 a month and adds VIP tiers, Shopify POS locations, unlimited integrations, a loyalty page design and Klaviyo loyalty flows built for you. Stores doing more than 500 orders a month are asked to get a quote for its Advanced or Plus plans.

**Best for:** brands that want help building the program, and larger stores ready for a custom plan.

### Yotpo Loyalty

The [Yotpo Loyalty listing](https://apps.shopify.com/swell) shows 4.7 stars from 965 reviews. The free plan is for stores with fewer than 100 orders a month (a rewards bar, points for purchases, discounts and a referral program). Pro is $199 a month and adds a rewards page, more ways to earn, more rewards, redeeming points at checkout and integrations such as Klaviyo and Recharge; from 500 orders a month it lists $0.08 to $0.20 per order. The listing mentions VIP tiers in its description but not on a plan card.

**Best for:** stores already using other Yotpo products, which the listing names among the apps it works with.

### CRO Toolbox

CRO Toolbox is new. It is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so it has no rating and no reviews, and you can't compare it on track record the way you can the apps above. Its [loyalty program](/features/loyalty-program/) has points for orders, signups, the first order, birthdays, product reviews and social follows; rewards as an amount or percent off, free shipping, a free product or store credit; a Points shop; referrals; and points on product pages, in the cart, in customer accounts, on the thank you page and at Shopify POS. It has 7 built in translations and supports right to left languages, and you can import members and points from a CSV file.

Loyalty is on the Growth plan ($19.99 a month, 500 orders that earn points a month) and the Pro plan ($39.99 a month, 2,000). Pro adds VIP tiers, bonus campaigns, product and collection rules, cashback, affiliate links, and Klaviyo, Omnisend, Judge.me and Shopify Flow. At the earning limit, orders stop earning until next month unless you allow extra on monthly billing ($5 per 100 more orders). Loyalty emails are coming soon and are not sent today.

The same plans also include upsells, bundles, cart offers and the Track and Returns pages. Growth covers up to 1,000 store orders a month and Pro has no orders limit. Go over the orders limit and you get 30 days to move up a plan; after that, new offers can't be switched on, and offers already on keep running. See [pricing](/pricing/).

**Best for:** small and mid sized stores that want VIP tiers without a $129 to $199 plan, or that would otherwise pay for a loyalty app and an upsell or tracking app side by side.

## When is another app the better choice?

- **You want proof today.** Smile and BON show thousands of reviews; CRO Toolbox has none yet.
- **You need loyalty emails now.** BON and Rivo list automated emails on their free plans; CRO Toolbox's are not live yet.
- **You have far more than 2,000 orders a month.** BON's Growth plan lists unlimited monthly orders, while CRO Toolbox Pro charges for extra earning orders above 2,000.
- **You want members to redeem points inside checkout on Shopify Plus.** Smile, BON and Yotpo list it on their plans.
- **You want a team to build the program for you.** LoyaltyLion's Classic plan lists onboarding and flows built for you.
- **Your current app works.** Moving members and points has a cost, even with a CSV import.
