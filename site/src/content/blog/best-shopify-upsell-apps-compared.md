The best Shopify upsell app is the one that shows offers where your shoppers decide (the product page, the cart, after payment), prices them correctly at checkout, and is billed in a way that still makes sense when your sales grow. Upsell apps are billed in very different ways: by plan, by store orders, or by the revenue the upsells bring in. We make CRO Toolbox, so read this as a guide with the facts we could check on each app's own listing, including the cases where another app is the better pick.

## What should an upsell app do?

At minimum:

- **Offers where they fit.** More of the same or a better version on the product page, matching products in the cart, one more item after payment.
- **The right price at checkout.** The discount on the offer must be the discount the shopper gets.
- **Offers that skip what makes no sense**, such as sold out products or products already in the cart.
- **A way to test** which offer earns more.
- **A look that fits your theme**, without editing code.

## Questions to ask before you install

1. **Where can it show offers?** Product page, cart drawer, checkout, after payment and the thank you page are different features.
2. **How is it billed?** A flat plan, store orders, or upsell revenue. Revenue based pricing grows as the app works.
3. **Is there a free plan for a live store**, or only for development stores?
4. **Does any part need Shopify Plus?** Upsells inside checkout do.
5. **Does it choose products for you**, or do you pick them?

## What does each app offer, and who is it best for?

### Aftersell

The [Aftersell listing](https://apps.shopify.com/aftersell), by Rokt, shows 4.9 stars from 1,128 reviews. Its free plan is only for development and partner stores. The Post Purchase plan ($34.99 a month) has one click offers after payment, smart funnels, thank you page offers, multivariate testing and upsells inside checkout (the listing marks those as Shopify Plus only). The Pre Purchase plan ($59.99) is an AI cart with 18+ blocks, rewards bars, free gifts, product page upsells and A/B testing. Complete ($79.99) has both and a recommendation engine. Each plan has a 30 day free trial. The listing also offers Rokt Thanks, third party offers on the confirmation page that it says earn up to $0.80 per order.

**Best for:** stores that want the most complete after payment funnels and a dedicated cart, and are happy to pay a flat price for them. Side by side: [CRO Toolbox vs AfterSell](/compare/cro-toolbox-vs-aftersell/).

### Selleasy

The [Selleasy listing](https://apps.shopify.com/upsell-cross-sell-kit-1), by Logbase, shows 4.9 stars from 2,790 reviews. The free plan has unlimited orders, up to 5 campaigns and 18 upsell and cross sell strategies. Starter is $9 a month with unlimited campaigns, Pro $29 with 1,000 AI recommendations a month and checkout upsells for Shopify Plus, and Max $59 with 2,500 AI recommendations and a rules engine. The listing covers frequently bought together, product page add ons, cart drawer upsells, after payment offers and thank you page recommendations. Paid plans have a 30 day free trial.

**Best for:** stores that want many offer types at a low flat price with no order limit, including a usable free plan. Side by side: [CRO Toolbox vs Selleasy](/compare/cro-toolbox-vs-selleasy/).

### ReConvert

The [ReConvert listing](https://apps.shopify.com/reconvert-upsell-cross-sell), now by Upsell.com, shows 4.8 stars from 3,139 reviews. The free plan covers up to 50 orders or $50 of upsell revenue and lists every feature: quantity breaks, upsell pop ups, A/B testing, AI recommendations, and offers on the product page, cart, checkout and after payment. Paid plans are priced by store orders or upsell revenue: $9.99 a month for 51 to 100, $19.99 for 101 to 200 and $39.99 for 201 to 500. Checkout upsells need Shopify Plus. Paid plans have a 14 day free trial.

**Best for:** smaller stores that want every placement, including after payment funnels, and a full featured free start.

### Zipify One Click Upsell

The [Zipify OCU listing](https://apps.shopify.com/zipify-oneclickupsell) shows 4.6 stars from 580 reviews and the Built for Shopify badge. Every paid tier has all features: product page offers, offers before and after payment, cart drawer upsells, thank you page upsells, AI upsells, A/B testing and checkout upsells on Shopify Plus. The price follows upsell revenue: $9.95 a month up to $200, $25 to $99 between $200 and $2,500, and from $149 above that, never past $999. The listing says you never pay for order volume. There is a 30 day free trial.

**Best for:** stores running paid traffic into after payment funnels, where paying by upsell revenue suits them.

### Rebuy

Rebuy's [App Store page](https://apps.shopify.com/rebuy) says the app is not currently available on the Shopify App Store, so there was no listing to check prices or reviews on. Our [CRO Toolbox vs Rebuy](/compare/cro-toolbox-vs-rebuy/) page has what Rebuy's own site said when we last checked it.

**Best for:** brands that want Rebuy's personalization engine; check Rebuy's own site for how to get it today.

### CRO Toolbox

CRO Toolbox is new. It is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so it has no rating and no reviews. Its [upsell offers](/features/upsell/) are quantity tiers such as Buy 2, save 10%, size upgrades and upgrade offers on the product page. [Cross sells](/features/cross-sell/) show on the product page, the cart page and the cart drawer, and can sit in your [theme's own cart drawer](/features/cart-offers/) (Dawn and Dawn based themes). There is also a one click [offer after payment](/features/post-purchase/), [thank you page](/features/thank-you/) modules, six kinds of bundles and discounts. On Pro, [A/B tests](/features/ab-tests/) show up to four versions of an upsell or cross sell, and offers can be scheduled.

You pick the products: products no rule covers fall back to Shopify's recommendations, and there is no AI recommendation engine. The offer after payment is one product at a discount, not a funnel of several offers, and there are no upsells inside the checkout itself.

The Free plan has three offers on at once and up to 50 orders a month, including the offer after payment. Starter ($5.99 a month) has unlimited offers and 500 orders, Growth ($19.99) 1,000 orders and offer results, and Pro ($39.99) no orders limit. None is billed by upsell revenue. Go over the orders limit and you get 30 days to move up a plan; after that, new offers can't be switched on, and offers already on keep running. See [pricing](/pricing/).

**Best for:** stores that want upsells, cross sells, bundles and an offer after payment at a flat price that doesn't grow with upsell revenue, alongside loyalty, tracking and returns in one app.

## When is another app the better choice?

- **You want proof today.** ReConvert and Selleasy show thousands of reviews; CRO Toolbox has none yet.
- **You want funnels after payment**, with more than one offer in a row. Aftersell (with downsells), ReConvert and Zipify OCU list funnels.
- **You are on Shopify Plus and want offers inside checkout.** Aftersell, Selleasy, ReConvert and Zipify OCU list checkout upsells for Plus.
- **You want the app to pick products for you.** Selleasy, ReConvert, Aftersell and Zipify OCU list AI recommendations.
- **You only want upsells.** Selleasy's free plan may be enough.
