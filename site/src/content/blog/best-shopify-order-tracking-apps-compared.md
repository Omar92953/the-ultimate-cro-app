The best Shopify order tracking app is the one that shows shoppers where their parcel is from the carriers you really use, sends the shipping updates you want, and is priced sensibly for the number of orders you ship each month. Most tracking apps charge by shipments or orders, so a busy month moves you up a plan. We make CRO Toolbox, so read this as a guide with the facts we could check on each app's own listing, including the cases where another app is the better pick.

## What should an order tracking app do?

At minimum:

- **A tracking page on your own store** where shoppers look up an order without writing to you.
- **Real updates from your carriers**, not only "shipped".
- **Shipping emails or texts**, if Shopify's own shipping emails are not enough for you.
- **Delivery dates** shoppers can trust.
- **Something useful after the status**, such as products to buy next, without hiding the status.

## Questions to ask before you install

1. **What is counted: shipments or orders?** One order can have several shipments.
2. **What happens over the plan's number?** Most apps listed below charge per extra order or shipment.
3. **Which carriers does it cover?** Check your local courier, not only the big names.
4. **Does it send its own notifications**, and on which plan?
5. **Do you dropship?** Some apps can hide where a parcel was sent from.

## What does each app offer, and who is it best for?

### AfterShip Order Tracking

The [AfterShip listing](https://apps.shopify.com/aftership) shows 4.6 stars from 1,514 reviews and the Built for Shopify badge. It lists 1,100+ carriers. The free plan has 50 shipments a month with a branded tracking page and the carrier's estimated delivery date. Essentials is $11 a month for 100 shipments ($0.08 per extra) and adds your logo, email and SMS notifications and reports. Premium is $70 for 500 shipments ($0.12 per extra) and adds a tracking page on your own domain, AI estimated delivery dates, product recommendations, analytics and an API. Paid plans cost $1 for the first month and have a 7 day free trial.

**Best for:** stores that want a well known tracking brand with shipment notifications and a dashboard that flags delivery problems.

### ParcelPanel, now CWILL Order Tracking

ParcelPanel's listing now goes by [CWILL Order Tracking](https://apps.shopify.com/parcelpanel) and says it was formerly ParcelPanel and ParcelWILL. It shows 4.9 stars from 3,211 reviews and the Built for Shopify badge, and lists 1,600+ carriers. The free plan has 20 orders a month, a branded and localized tracking page and a dropshipping mode. Essential is $11 for 200 orders and adds shipping notifications, an estimated delivery date after purchase, AI product recommendations and discount upsells. Professional is $59 for 2,000 orders (Klaviyo and PayPal integrations, an API, no CWILL branding), and Enterprise $479 for 25,000. Extra orders cost $0.05 on every paid plan.

**Best for:** stores that want tracking plus selling on the tracking page at a low price, and dropshipping stores.

### Track123

The [Track123 listing](https://apps.shopify.com/track123) shows 4.9 stars from 1,731 reviews. The free plan has 50 orders a month with branded multilingual tracking pages, Shopify's own shipping emails, a dropshipping mode and widgets for the order status page and customer accounts. Growth is $9 for 300 orders and adds product recommendations, Apple Wallet tracking and custom shipping notifications. Advanced is $49 for 2,000 orders and adds estimated delivery times, AI recommendations and Klaviyo, Omnisend, Slack and Gorgias integrations. Extra orders cost $0.05.

**Best for:** small stores that want a generous free plan and a map based tracking page, and dropshipping stores.

### 17TRACK Order Tracking

The [17TRACK listing](https://apps.shopify.com/17track) shows 4.9 stars from 4,605 reviews, the most of the apps here, and lists 3,300+ carriers. The free plan has 50 shipments a month with a branded tracking page, PayPal integration and a dropshipping mode. Basic is $9 for 200 shipments and adds email notifications. Pro is $49 for 1,000 shipments and adds multilingual pages and emails and estimated delivery dates. Premium is $159 for 2,000 shipments and adds delivery analysis.

**Best for:** stores shipping internationally with many different carriers, and dropshipping stores.

### CRO Toolbox

CRO Toolbox is new. It is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so it has no rating and no reviews. Its [Track order page](/features/track/) is part of a bigger app rather than a tracking app on its own. Shoppers look up their order with the order number and email and see the status, the tracking number, the carrier's tracking link, and the estimated and delivered dates from Shopify. For the parcel's full history you connect your own key from 17TRACK, AfterShip, TrackingMore or Ship24 (an account with that service), a webhook link from your shipping company, or type updates for a local courier.

What it adds is selling on the page: a real discount code for the next order, a Buy again button, product recommendations, a message after delivery, and an apology with an optional code for late orders. On Pro there are offers above and below the tracking. CRO Toolbox doesn't send its own shipping emails or texts; Shopify's shipping emails keep doing that.

The Track page is on the Growth plan ($19.99 a month) and the Pro plan ($39.99), with no limit on shipments tracked with your own key, and the same plans include upsells, bundles, a Returns page and loyalty. Growth covers up to 1,000 store orders a month and Pro has no orders limit. Go over the orders limit and you get 30 days to move up a plan; after that, new offers can't be switched on, and offers already on keep running. Until Shopify approves the app's access to all orders, orders older than 60 days can't be looked up. See [pricing](/pricing/).

**Best for:** stores that are happy with Shopify's shipping emails and want a tracking page that sells, in the same app as their offers and returns.

## When is another app the better choice?

- **You want proof today.** 17TRACK and ParcelPanel show thousands of reviews; CRO Toolbox has none yet.
- **You want shipping emails or texts from the tracking app.** AfterShip, ParcelPanel, Track123 and 17TRACK all list notifications on their paid plans.
- **You want carrier tracking without a second account.** The apps above track carriers themselves; for full parcel history CRO Toolbox needs your own key from a tracking service.
- **You want a shipments dashboard and delivery analytics.** AfterShip and 17TRACK list them.
- **You only need tracking.** A free or $9 plan from a tracking app may be all you need.
