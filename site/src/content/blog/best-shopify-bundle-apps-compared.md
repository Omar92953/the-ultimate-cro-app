The best Shopify bundle app is the one that supports the bundles you actually sell, prices them correctly at checkout, keeps your stock right and costs a sensible amount for your order volume. We make CRO Toolbox, so read this as a guide to the questions to ask, with links to side by side comparisons that cite their sources.

## What should a bundle app do?

At minimum:

- **The bundle types you need.** Fixed sets, kits sold as one product, mix and match boxes, tiered or growing discounts, any N for one price.
- **The right price at checkout.** The price on the page must be the price the shopper pays.
- **Stock for each item.** When a bundle sells, each item's stock should go down.
- **A builder that fits your theme.** Ideally without editing theme code.

## Questions to ask before you install

1. **Which bundle types does it support?** Many apps do one or two well.
2. **How is pricing billed?** By plan, by orders, by bundle revenue or by usage. Usage based pricing can grow quickly as you sell more.
3. **What happens over a limit?** Some apps stop showing bundles above a cap; others only remind you.
4. **Does the bundle split into items at checkout?** That's what keeps stock and fulfilment right.
5. **Does any part need Shopify Plus?** Ask before you plan a feature around it.

## When is another app the better choice?

- You only need one bundle type and like that app's plans.
- You need something CRO Toolbox doesn't do today.
- You already use an app that works well for your store; switching has a cost.

## The short version

List the bundles you want to sell, check each app supports them and prices them at checkout, compare what you'd pay at your order volume, and test with a real order before you launch.
