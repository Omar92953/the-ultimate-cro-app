The best bundle idea is the set your customers already buy together, sold in the way they like to buy it. Beauty shoppers buy routines, candle shoppers buy gifts, coffee drinkers buy a brewer with its filters. Below are bundle ideas by industry, each matched to one of the six bundle types in CRO Toolbox, with what to watch for.

## What are the six bundle types?

CRO Toolbox has six ways to sell products together on Shopify. Each has its own page with setup steps:

- [Frequently bought together](/bundles/fbt/): a set of items shown on each item's page, with a discount when they're bought together.
- [Fixed bundle](/bundles/kit/): a ready made set with its own page, picture and price; the cart shows it as one line.
- [Mix and match](/bundles/mixmatch/): shoppers pick items from a collection on the bundle's own page, for one price.
- [Build your own bundle](/bundles/browse/): shoppers add items from collection pages while a tray follows them.
- [Tiered bundle](/bundles/growing/): the more items shoppers add, the bigger the discount.
- [Fixed price bundle](/bundles/any-n/): any 3 for $30, any 6 for $55, picked from a long collection.

The question for each idea below is the same: does the shopper want to choose, or do they want it chosen for them? A fixed set suits shoppers who want it chosen. A box to fill suits shoppers who know what they like.

## Beauty and skincare: a routine kit

**Bundle type:** [Fixed bundle](/bundles/kit/).

Skincare sells as routines: cleanser, serum, moisturiser. A fixed bundle sells the whole routine as one product with its own picture, price and "What's inside" list. The app creates the product with Shopify's own bundles, and Shopify tracks its stock from its items, so selling a kit takes each item from stock.

Ideas:

- A morning routine and an evening routine, each as one kit
- A starter kit in travel sizes
- A gift set for the holidays

**Watch for:** a fixed bundle always has the same items. If shoppers want to pick their own products, a [Tiered bundle](/bundles/growing/) from your skincare collection fits better, for example 2 items 10% off and 3 items 15% off. More ideas on the [bundles for beauty and skincare brands](/use-cases/bundles-for-beauty-brands/) page.

## Candles and home: a pick 3 box

**Bundle type:** [Mix and match](/bundles/mixmatch/).

Candle and home shoppers often buy gifts and like to choose scents themselves. A mix and match box lets them fill it from a collection, with steps such as "Pick 3 candles" (min 3, max 3). You can price it as one price for the box, a percentage off the picks, or tiers. A step can be optional, such as "Add a gift box".

Ideas:

- Pick any 3 candles for one price
- A candle, a diffuser and a room spray, one from each collection
- A seasonal box with an optional matches step

**Watch for:** keep the collection to products that really go in the box. Full guide: [mix and match bundles on Shopify](/blog/mix-and-match-bundles-on-shopify/), and the [home and candle stores](/use-cases/home-and-candle-stores/) page.

## Coffee and kitchen: a set on the product page

**Bundle type:** [Frequently bought together](/bundles/fbt/).

Coffee and kitchen products often need a companion: beans with filters, a pour over with a kettle, a pan with a lid. A Frequently bought together box shows two or three items on each item's product page, with a discount when the whole set is in the cart. Shoppers don't have to add everything at once: the discount applies at checkout when the set is complete, however the items were added.

Ideas:

- Pour over, paper filters and a bag of beans
- A knife with a sharpener
- A mug with a matching saucer

**Watch for:** keep sets to two or three items that clearly belong together. A product can be in two sets, but keep them different so the page doesn't feel crowded. Step by step: [how to add Frequently bought together to a Shopify theme](/blog/how-to-add-frequently-bought-together-to-shopify/).

## Apparel and basics: any 3 for one price

**Bundle type:** [Fixed price bundle](/bundles/any-n/).

Socks, tees and underwear are bought in multiples, and a round price is easy to understand. A fixed price bundle sets a price for a number of items, for example any 3 pairs for $30 and any 6 for $55 (example prices). Long collections get Show more and a search box in the builder, so it works with a big range of colours and prints.

Ideas:

- Any 3 pairs of socks for one price
- Any 5 basic tees
- Any 3 hair accessories

**Watch for:** if the offer is several of one product in different sizes, quantity breaks may be simpler. With **Let shoppers pick a size for each item** on, Buy 2 can be one M and one L. See [upsells for fashion and apparel stores](/use-cases/upsells-for-fashion/).

## Pets: buy more, save more

**Bundle type:** [Tiered bundle](/bundles/growing/).

Pet owners buy treats, chews and toys again and again, and often try several flavours. A tiered bundle grows the discount with the number of items: for example 2 items 10% off, 3 items 15%, 4 items 20%. The builder shows a line such as "Add 1 more to save 15%", so shoppers can see the next step.

Ideas:

- Treats and chews from one collection, more off for each extra bag
- A toy bundle with a tier for 3 and for 5
- Grooming products for one type of pet

**Watch for:** a tiered bundle mixes different products. If shoppers mostly buy more of the same food, a quantity break on that product is clearer.

## Gifts and big catalogues: build it while browsing

**Bundle type:** [Build your own bundle](/bundles/browse/).

Gift shops and stores with large catalogues have too many products for one builder page. With Build your own bundle, shoppers add items from collection pages ("+ Add to bundle" on every product card) and from product pages, while the bundle tray follows them with each step's progress. Once the bundle is complete, the tray shows **Add bundle to cart** or **Checkout**.

Ideas:

- A gift tote: one item from each of three collections
- A care package: a snack, a card and a small present
- A stationery set: a notebook, a pen and a sticker pack

**Watch for:** this needs the **Bundle tray** app embed on. Adding the **Add to bundle** block to product pages is optional. For a small range, Mix and match is simpler for shoppers.

## Food and drink: mixed cases

Food and drink stores can use two of the types above: a [Fixed price bundle](/bundles/any-n/) for any 6 from a long collection, or a [Mix and match](/bundles/mixmatch/) box. See [mixed cases for food and drink stores](/use-cases/food-and-drink-mixed-cases/).

## How do I create a bundle in CRO Toolbox?

1. Open **Bundles → Create bundle offer**. The app asks what you want to sell and opens the right editor.
2. Choose one of the six bundle types.
3. Follow the numbered steps on the left: items or collections, price, and look. The live preview on the right uses your real products and prices.
4. Save. A step with a problem opens itself so you can fix it.
5. Put it on your store. Mix and match, Tiered and Fixed price bundles need **Add to theme** once. Frequently bought together shows through the **Conversion boosters** app embed. Build your own bundle needs the **Bundle tray** app embed. A Fixed bundle is its own product, created by the app. See [turn on the app embeds](/help/turn-on-app-embeds/).

Customers pay exactly the bundle's price. At checkout a bundle the shopper built is split into the items they picked, so each item's stock goes down and the items appear on the order. More detail in the [bundles help guide](/help/bundles/) and on [Shopify bundles](/shopify-bundles/).

## Which plan do I need?

Every bundle type is on the Free plan, which allows 3 offers on at once and 50 orders a month. Starter, at $5.99 a month, has unlimited offers on at once. See [pricing](/pricing/) and the [bundles feature page](/features/bundles/).

## Quick checklist

- Start from what customers already buy together
- Fixed set for "choose for me", a box for "let me choose"
- Two or three items for Frequently bought together
- A clear price: one price, a percentage off or tiers
- The right app embed or block turned on
- A test order to check the price and each item's stock
