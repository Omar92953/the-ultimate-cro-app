To add back in stock alerts on Shopify, replace the dead end of a Sold out button with a "Notify me" form that asks for the shopper's email for that exact product and size. Keep a list of who is waiting for what, and when the stock comes back, email those people with a link to the product. For items you will restock on a known date, you can take pre orders instead and keep selling. Here is how to set up each part.

## Does Shopify have back in stock alerts built in?

No. Shopify themes show a Sold out button when a product or size has no stock, but Shopify doesn't collect "tell me when it's back" requests on its own. You need an app or a form for that. Without one, a shopper who wanted the item simply leaves, and you never learn how many people wanted it.

## What should a Notify me form ask?

- **Only the email.** Every extra field loses people.
- **The exact size or colour.** A request for "the blue one in medium" is worth more than one for the product, because that is the variant you restock.
- **A clear promise.** "We'll email you once when it's back" tells the shopper what they are signing up for.

Only add these shoppers to your newsletter if they agreed to marketing emails. Asking to hear about one product is not the same as subscribing.

## How do I tell shoppers when the product is back?

1. Restock the product in Shopify.
2. Find everyone waiting for that product and size.
3. Send a short email soon after: the product name and photo, a link straight to the product page, and an honest note if stock is limited. Shopify Email or the email tool you already use works well for this.
4. Mark those people as told, so nobody gets the same email twice.

Send it quickly. The shopper asked because they wanted the item then; a week later they may have bought it elsewhere.

## Should I take pre orders instead?

Pre orders work when you know the item is coming back and roughly when. In Shopify, open the product, and under the variant's inventory turn on **Continue selling when out of stock**. Shoppers can then buy it at zero stock.

Two things matter with pre orders:

- **Say it clearly.** The button and the product page should say "Pre order" and when it ships, so nobody expects it tomorrow.
- **The shopper pays now.** Shopify charges at checkout as usual, so only take pre orders you are sure you can fulfil, and tell customers early if the date moves.

Use a Notify me form when you are not sure the item comes back; use pre orders when you are.

## Quick checklist

- A Notify me form on every sold out product, asking only for an email
- Requests saved per size and colour, not just per product
- Marketing emails only for shoppers who agreed
- An email to everyone waiting soon after you restock, with a direct link
- People marked as told once you have emailed them
- Pre orders only for items you know are coming back, with the ship date shown
