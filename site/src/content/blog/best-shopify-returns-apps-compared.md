The best Shopify returns app is the one that lets shoppers start a return without emailing you, turns as many refunds as it can into exchanges or store credit, handles labels the way you ship, and costs a sensible amount for the number of returns you get. Most returns apps price by returns per month, so the plan you need depends on your return rate as much as on your sales. We make CRO Toolbox, so read this as a guide with the facts we could check on each app's own listing, including the cases where another app is the better pick.

## What should a returns app do?

At minimum:

- **A returns page on your store** where shoppers pick the items and a reason.
- **Rules** for the return window, items that can't come back and reasons you approve at once.
- **Exchanges and store credit** offered before a refund, so you keep the sale.
- **Return labels**, generated for you or uploaded by you.
- **A real Shopify return** behind each request, so stock, refunds and reports stay right.

## Questions to ask before you install

1. **How many returns does the plan include, and what does each extra one cost?**
2. **Which plan has store credit and exchanges?** It is often not the first paid plan.
3. **Does it make labels for you**, and with which carriers?
4. **Does it connect to your warehouse or 3PL**, if you use one?
5. **Can shoppers exchange for any product** ("shop now"), or only another size or colour?

## What does each app offer, and who is it best for?

### ReturnGO

The [ReturnGO listing](https://apps.shopify.com/returngo) shows 4.9 stars from 410 reviews and pricing from $147 a month, with no free plan. Premium ($147) includes 110 returns a month, then $1.25 per extra return, with product and variant exchanges, Shop Now exchanges, return labels through Ship by ReturnGO, store credit and gift cards, and auto approval. Pro ($297) includes 300 returns, then $0.90 each, and adds advanced automation, ERP integrations, unlimited languages and an API. Both have a 14 day free trial.

**Best for:** stores with a steady volume of returns that want an exchange first returns platform with labels and integrations ready, and can justify the price.

### Return Prime

The [Return Prime listing](https://apps.shopify.com/return-prime) shows 4.8 stars from 780 reviews. It is free to install with 5 return requests, then $0.49 per request; the free plan already has a branded portal, variant exchanges and an EU withdrawal button. Grow starts at $19.99 a month for 60 requests and adds Shop Now exchanges, instant exchanges and refunds to store credit. Thrive AI is $79.99 for 180 requests (fraud controls, smart exchange discounts and return fees), and Scale $149.99 for 450+ requests with the warehouse system of your choice. Paid plans have a 15 day free trial.

**Best for:** small and mid sized stores that want store credit and exchanges at a low starting price, and stores selling in the EU.

### Loop Returns

The [Loop listing](https://apps.shopify.com/loop-returns) shows 4.6 stars from 450 reviews. Its Checkout+ plan is listed as free for brands with 5k+ annual orders and includes returns software, US and Canada domestic return labels and package protection. The Returns plan starts at $340 a month, includes up to 1,000 returns a year, and adds Shop Now, instant exchanges, bonus credit and fraud prevention.

**Best for:** larger direct to consumer brands in the US and Canada that want a full returns and exchanges operation.

### AfterShip Returns & Exchanges

The [AfterShip Returns listing](https://apps.shopify.com/returns-center-by-aftership) shows 4.7 stars from 1,546 reviews and the Built for Shopify badge. Return Care is free: shoppers pay for their returns and AfterShip handles the labels on a revenue share. Essentials is $19 a month for 20 returns ($0.50 per extra) with automatic labels, a branded returns page, automatic approval, refunds and exchanges. Premium is $119 for 100 returns ($1 per extra) and adds AI exchanges, fraud prevention and a warranty portal. Paid plans cost $1 for the first month and have a 7 day free trial.

**Best for:** stores that want labels made for them, and stores that already use AfterShip for tracking.

### CRO Toolbox

CRO Toolbox is new. It is not on the Shopify App Store yet, so it has no rating and no reviews. Its [Returns page](/features/returns/) lets shoppers ask for a return with the order number and email; each request becomes a real Shopify return you approve or decline, and Shopify emails the shopper. You set the return window, products that can't be returned, reasons approved at once and your return address. You upload the return label for the shopper and refund when the items arrive. It doesn't make labels or connect to carriers or warehouses.

On Pro, [return saving rules](/features/return-saving/) offer store credit (with an optional bonus) or an exchange for another size or colour before the refund, approve returns by rule, and put returned items back in stock. Credit goes to the customer's Shopify store credit, so it needs a customer account on the order. Refunds to the original payment stay available.

The Returns page is on the Growth plan ($19.99 a month); store credit, exchanges and approval by rule are on Pro ($39.99). Neither plan charges per return, and the same plans include upsells, bundles, a Track page and loyalty. Growth covers up to 1,000 store orders a month and Pro has no orders limit. Go over the orders limit and you get 30 days to move up a plan; after that, new offers can't be switched on, and offers already on keep running. See [pricing](/pricing/).

**Best for:** stores that create their own labels and want store credit and exchanges without a per return fee, in the same app as their offers.

## When is another app the better choice?

- **You want proof today.** AfterShip Returns and Return Prime show hundreds to thousands of reviews; CRO Toolbox has none yet.
- **You want labels made for you.** ReturnGO, Loop and AfterShip Returns list labels; with CRO Toolbox you upload your own.
- **You use a warehouse, 3PL or ERP.** ReturnGO and Return Prime list those integrations.
- **You want exchanges for any product, fraud checks or a warranty portal.** Shop Now, fraud controls and warranties are on the listings of the specialists, not in CRO Toolbox.
- **You get very few returns.** Return Prime's free plan or a small AfterShip plan may cost less than any plan with other tools included.
