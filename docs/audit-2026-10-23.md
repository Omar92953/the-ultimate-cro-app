## 1. Security (public routes)

Read: every `proxy.*`, `webhooks.*`, `flow.*`, `email.*`, `tracking.hook.*`, `api.*`, `mcp.*` route and the helpers they call.
Every route checks its signature or token before any work: app proxy routes use `authenticate.public.appProxy`
(Shopify's signature, `logged_in_customer_id` comes from Shopify); webhooks use `authenticate.webhook`; Flow checks
Shopify's HMAC (`flowSigned`, constant time); Postmark checks a key derived from the app secret; the tracking hook a per
store secret (`sameSecret`, constant time, never empty); post-purchase verifies Shopify's HS256 token; checkout and POS
use `authenticate.public.checkout` / `authenticate.pos`; MCP uses hashed bearer tokens tied to one shop. Order lookup
needs the order number and that order's own email (exact match). Emails escape every value (`escHtml`), the accent is
a validated colour and the logo must be https.

## 3. Money and points

Read: the loyalty ledger (`record`, `syncOrder`, `releasePending`, `expireInactive`, `redeem`), refunds and cancels,
referrals and affiliates, reward codes and store credit, usage limits and billing (`allowUnit`, `reportUsage`), the
Function metafield size checks. Sound: every point change goes through `record` in one transaction (points and pending
always equal the ledger sums); spending is all or nothing in one update (two clicks can't overspend) and points come
back when Shopify refuses; refunds of pending points stay pending and are released with the order; each order, refund,
referral and reward is deduplicated; referrals refuse own codes, repeat friends, the monthly cap and non first orders,
and a refund takes the reward back; reward codes are one use, for that customer only, with an end date; each unit over
a limit is stored once (unique key) and sent to Shopify with an idempotency key, so nothing is billed twice.

## 4. Plans

Admin saves follow the plan in one place: `app/shopify.server.ts` runs `requirePage` on every non GET admin request,
and every admin page with a save is mapped to its feature in `catalog.ts` (checked by listing every `app.*` route with an
action against `featuresForPath`). Inside pages: offer slots, checkout rules, countdown places and thank you modules are
counted (`checkLimit`, `checkCount`), Track page offers and the holdout test have their own checks, loyalty runs with
`planned` (Pro parts kept but not applied on Growth), and a downgrade switches off what is over the counted limits
without deleting it (`applyPlanLimits`). Free and Starter see an upgrade card instead of a locked page.
