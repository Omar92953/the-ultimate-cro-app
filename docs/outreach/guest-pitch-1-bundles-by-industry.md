## Bundle ideas for 8 kinds of Shopify store

A bundle works when it saves the shopper a decision, not only money. The best bundles group products
people already buy together, at a price where the saving is easy to see. Below are ideas for eight
kinds of store, with the bundle type that fits each one.

**The four bundle types, briefly**

- **Fixed kit:** always the same items, one product, one price. Shopify's own bundles can track its
  stock from the items.
- **Mix and match:** the shopper fills a box from a collection ("pick 3 candles").
- **Frequently bought together:** two or three items shown on each item's product page, with a discount
  when all are in the cart.
- **Any N for one price:** "any 3 pairs for $30", good for big catalogues of similar items.

**Beauty and skincare.** A routine kit (cleanser, serum, cream) as a fixed kit, because the order of use
matters and the contents don't change. Add a travel size version for gifts.

**Candles and home.** Mix and match: "any 3 candles", so shoppers pick scents. Price the box so three
cost noticeably less than three singles.

**Coffee and tea.** Frequently bought together: beans with a filter or a mug. A tiered option works for
subscribers who stock up: 2 bags 10% off, 3 bags 15% off.

**Apparel.** Any N for one price on basics (tees, socks, underwear), with a size picker per item. Avoid
bundles that force one size for all items.

**Pets.** A starter kit for new owners (bowl, lead, treats) as a fixed kit; treats as "any 4 for one
price".

**Stationery.** Mix and match notebooks and pens, with optional steps such as "add a pen case".

**Food and snacks.** A tasting box (fixed) for first time buyers, then "build your own box" for repeat
buyers.

**Gifts.** A gift set with a box, and a free gift wrap step. Show the full value of the items and the
bundle price side by side.

**Before you launch any bundle**

1. Check the price at checkout, not only on the product page.
2. Check that each item's stock goes down when the bundle sells.
3. Check how discount codes combine with the bundle discount.
4. Place one test order and look at what your fulfilment team will see.

**Measure it.** Compare average order value and the share of orders that contain a bundle, month on
month. Keep the bundles that sell and drop the rest.
