Most free shipping bars do the same core job, so choose on these three things rather than on the name:

1. It reads the real cart total and updates live in the cart drawer and on the cart page, not only in a banner at the top of the store.
2. The goal amount matches the free shipping you actually give in Shopify (a free rate over a set amount in Settings > Shipping and delivery, or an automatic free shipping discount), so the bar never promises something checkout doesn't give.
3. It handles discounts and other currencies correctly and doesn't slow the store down.

Before you pick an app, pick the number. Set the threshold a little above your average order value, close to the price of an item shoppers often add. If most of your orders are already above it, you are paying for shipping without getting bigger orders.

Shopify can give free shipping over an amount on its own, but it doesn't show shoppers how close they are. That progress message is what the bar apps add.
