**Title (only if starting a thread):** How to sell bundles on Shopify without overselling stock

**Answer:**

There are three common ways to sell a bundle on Shopify, and the main difference is what happens to
stock.

1. **A separate product with its own stock.** Quick to set up, but its stock isn't linked to the items
   inside, so you have to adjust both by hand. Fine for a small kit you assemble ahead of time.
2. **Shopify's own bundles (fixed bundles).** The bundle is a product made of other products. Shopify
   works out its stock from the items and takes each item from stock when it sells. Good when the
   contents never change.
3. **A bundle the shopper builds** (mix and match, "any 3 for $30", tiers). This needs an app. Check
   that the app splits the bundle into the real items at checkout, so each item's stock goes down and
   your fulfilment team sees what to pack.

A few things to test before you launch, whichever route you choose:

- Place a test order and check the order page: are the items listed one by one?
- Check the price at checkout, not only on the product page.
- Check how a discount code combines with the bundle price (Settings → Discounts, combinations).
- If you ship by weight, check the bundle's weight.
