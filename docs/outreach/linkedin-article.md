**Title:** Why I put bundles, upsells and cross sells into one Shopify app

**Article:**

Running a Shopify store, I ended up with one app per job: one for bundles, one for upsells, one for the
free shipping bar, one for order tracking, one for returns. Each had its own settings, its own styles,
its own bill, and its own script on every product page.

So I built CRO Toolbox. In one line: CRO Toolbox is a Shopify app to increase average order value (AOV) and lifetime value (LTV).

**What I wanted it to do differently**

- **Real discounts at checkout.** Bundle prices and offer discounts are Shopify discounts, so the price
  the shopper sees is the price they pay.
- **Stock that stays right.** A bundle the shopper builds is split into its items at checkout, so each
  item's stock goes down and the order shows what to pack.
- **No theme code.** Everything is a theme app block or embed you place in the theme editor, on any
  Online Store 2.0 theme.
- **One look.** Offers share the store's style settings instead of each app bringing its own.
- **The pages after the sale count too.** A Track your order page and a returns page, both made by the
  app, and a thank you page that invites the next order.

**What's in it today**

Six bundle types, quantity breaks and size upgrades, cross sells on the product page and in the cart,
a one click offer after payment, a free shipping bar, a discount code box in the cart, reviews, a
wishlist, a size guide, payment and delivery rules, and analytics for each offer.

**Pricing**

A free plan, then $5.99, $19.99 or $39.99 a month, each with a 14 day free trial.

**What I learned**

Most stores don't need more offers; they need one clear offer in the right place. One offer per
product page, a free shipping goal set from real numbers, and a measured test beat a page full of
pop ups.
