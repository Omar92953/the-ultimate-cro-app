# Shopify Community answer 2: setting a free shipping threshold

**Where:** https://community.shopify.com, search "free shipping threshold", "free shipping over",
"free shipping bar". Answer a recent thread without an accepted answer.

**Title (only if starting a thread):** How do you pick the right free shipping threshold?

**Answer:**

A simple way to set it is to start from your own numbers rather than a round figure:

1. **Find your average order value** (Analytics → Reports → Average order value, last 90 days).
2. **Find what shipping costs you per order** on average.
3. **Set the threshold a little above your average order**, so a typical shopper needs one small extra
   item to reach it. If the threshold is far above most orders, few shoppers will try to reach it.
4. **Check the maths.** For example (made up numbers): if your average order is $40, shipping costs you
   $6 and your margin is 50%, a $50 threshold means a shopper who adds $10 brings $5 of extra margin
   but costs you $6 of shipping. That's a loss on that order unless the extra item has a better margin
   or the threshold sits higher. Run it with your own figures.
5. **Show the progress.** A line in the cart such as "You're $8 away from free shipping" helps shoppers
   see how close they are. Suggest a product that closes the gap.
6. **Set it up in Shopify**: Settings → Shipping and delivery → your rates → add a free rate with a
   minimum order price. The bar only shows the goal; the free rate is what removes the charge.

Review it after a month: did average order value move, and did the share of orders just above the
threshold grow?
