**Name:** CRO Toolbox

**Pricing:** Free (free plan; paid plans with a free trial)

**Description (260 max):**
CRO Toolbox is a Shopify app to increase average order value (AOV) and lifetime value (LTV). Six bundle types, quantity breaks,
cart offers, a one click offer after payment, a free shipping bar, reviews, an order tracking page and
returns. Any Online Store 2.0 theme, no code.

**Gallery:** `branding/app-store/feature-banner.png` first, then the six listing screenshots in
`branding/old/screenshots/listing/` (same order as `docs/listing.md`). Optional: a 30 to 60 second screen
recording of the mix and match demo at https://crotoolbox.com/demo/.

**Maker's first comment:**

Hi Product Hunt! I'm Omar. I run a Shopify store and kept stacking apps: one for bundles, one for
upsells, one for the free shipping bar, one for tracking, one for returns. CRO Toolbox does those jobs
in one app.

A few things it does differently:
- Bundle and offer prices are real Shopify discounts, so the checkout price matches the page.
- Bundles a shopper builds are split into their items at checkout, so stock stays right.
- Everything is a theme block you place in the theme editor; your theme's code is never edited.
- The Track your order and Returns pages are made for you, and returns become real Shopify returns.

There's a free plan; paid plans are $5.99, $19.99 and $39.99 a month with a 14 day trial.
I'd love feedback, especially on what you'd need before switching from the apps you use now.
