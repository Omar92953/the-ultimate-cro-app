# Demo product photos: sources and licences

The product pictures in `site/public/products/` (used by the live demos at crotoolbox.com/demo/ and by
the website's product pictures, see `site/public/products/README.md`) are free stock photos from
**Burst**, Shopify's free photo site (https://www.shopify.com/stock-photos). Picked and checked on
2026-10-20 by the website session.

## Licences (checked 2026-10-20)

Each photo's own page names its licence. Both kinds allow what the demos need:

- **Burst Some Rights Reserved** (https://www.shopify.com/stock-photos/licenses/shopify-some-rights-reserved):
  free for commercial and noncommercial use, adapting allowed, no attribution needed. Not allowed:
  selling the photos as photo files, compiling them into a service that competes with Burst, or
  claiming to be the author.
- **CC0** (https://www.shopify.com/stock-photos/licenses/creative-commons): Creative Commons Zero, free
  for commercial and noncommercial use, no attribution needed.

## Rules used when picking

- No people, hands or faces; no brands, logos or readable text on the product.
- Square crop, 1200 x 1200 px, WebP, under 200 KB, product roughly centred.
- One file per product kind (the default), plus a file per product handle when that product needs its
  own picture (the six candles, the socks, the gift boxes). Handles are in `demos/src/data.mjs`; some
  sock titles were renamed to match their photo (handles unchanged).

## Changing a photo

1. Pick a photo on Burst (or another source whose licence allows free commercial use without
   attribution) that follows the rules above.
2. Crop it to 1200 x 1200 and save it as WebP under 200 KB with the same file name.
3. Update its row here (photo link, licence, size, date in the heading).
4. `npm --prefix demos run build`, `npm run test:storefront`, then `node demos/screenshots.mjs` to
   refresh the website screenshots.
